image-music

Pictures, played as sound.

Everything here runs entirely in the browser — no build step, no server, nothing uploaded. On a phone, start with pad mode.

start here · works on phones

Pad mode

Take a photo and it becomes one sustained chord — colour picks the harmony, texture picks the detune, brightness picks the filter. Export it as a seamless loop, a one-shot and a MIDI chord.

 bench

The sequencer bench

The original mapping, where the image is read as a score: left→right is time, bottom→top is pitch. Includes the angle lab that measures how much a tilted camera moves the derived key.

 prototype

Sequencer prototype

The modular version of the same engine — separate analysis, mapping, synthesis and scheduling files, meant to be read rather than demoed.

 experiment

Pocket Cat Dex

A sibling idea: photograph a cat, get a 32×32 pixel card whose stats are read off the actual pixels, and collect them.
